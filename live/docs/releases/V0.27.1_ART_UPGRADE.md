# Critter Extraction v0.27.1 Art Upgrade

This release converts the raw-WebGL art from shared block-and-sphere assemblies into clearer cartoon-realistic silhouettes while preserving offline play, WebGL1, Canvas2D fallback rendering, all hitboxes, combat math, movement, saves, and network payloads.

## Procedural primitives

| Primitive | Purpose | Low / Medium / High | Canvas2D fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cone` | Tapered trees, muzzles, barrels, foliage, carrot shells | 6 / 10 / 16 sides | Rotated four-point taper |
| `capsule` | Organic torsos, limbs, paws, tails, bottles | 2×6 / 3×10 / 5×16 cap detail | Rounded rectangle |
| `wedge` | Ears, stocks, armor leaves, capes, windmill blades | Fixed 8 triangles | Rotated triangle |
| `crystal` | Gems, energy cores, ice, Moon Slugs | 5 / 6 / 8 sides | Faceted six-point gem |

The renderer registers fixed wedges once and creates the other meshes per graphics tier. Low quality keeps the smallest segment counts and reduced decorative pieces.

## Shading

The shared WebGL1 fragment shader now uses a three-band diffuse ramp, stronger fresnel rim, restrained hemisphere light, emissive support, and the existing distance fog. It adds no textures, texture lookups, or runtime assets.

## Asset redesign coverage

- All eight critters now have species-specific ears, muzzles, cheeks, tails, markings, body proportions, and first-person paw details.
- All five weapons have distinct silhouettes in third person, first person, and world pickups.
- Every healing item, utility item, valuable, armor set, ammo family, and objective pickup has its own procedural model.
- Pine trees use real tapered canopy layers. Broadleaf scenery uses organic capsule clusters.
- Silo and windmill roofs taper correctly; windmill blades use wedges; Frostflower crystal landmarks and ice shards use faceted crystal geometry; redwood landmarks use tapered trunks and crowns.

## Revertible rollout phases

1. Primitives, renderer registration, Canvas2D branches, and shader.
2. Critter bodies, eight species silhouettes, markings, and first-person paws.
3. Five third-person and first-person weapon families.
4. World weapon models and every item, armor, and ammo pickup.
5. Trees, broadleaf scenery, and regional landmarks.

Each phase is visually isolated. No current-code gaps were encountered, so the upgrade does not contain guessed gameplay or networking changes.
