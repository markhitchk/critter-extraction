Inventory & Extraction

Open chests with E, manage loot in the grid backpack, and watch the weight limit. Carry at least five Moonberries to the cyan beacon and hold E for two seconds. Extracted items move into your account stash; failed-drop loot is lost.

Co-op

Host and guest exchange two long WebRTC codes. Once connected, game state travels directly between the two browsers. Restrictive networks may still require a TURN relay, which this backend-free build does not include.
