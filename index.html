Touch Screens

Phones and tablets automatically get a movement joystick, a right-side drag area for continuous 360° looking, and large controls for jump, fire, aim, reload, interact, healing, crouch, backpack, camera, shoulder, and pause. Touchscreen laptops stay in mouse/keyboard mode unless touch controls are forced in Settings.

Hold FIRE, AIM, or USE while moving and looking with other fingers. Tap C to toggle crouch. Landscape orientation gives the clearest view.

Contracts & Extraction

Each run rolls a required drop contract and an optional bonus contract from combat, scavenging, exploration, survival, and gear objectives. Locate the beacon, complete the required contract, carry at least five Moonberries, then hold E or USE at the beacon for two seconds. Extracted items move into your stash; failed-drop loot is lost.

Multiplayer & Browsers

One host and up to three guests join with a six-digit room code. A shared GitHub Pages URL only opens the Join Multiplayer screen; it does not contain, prefill, or submit the PIN. Every guest must manually type the host's room code and press Join Room. The host chooses Co-op Extraction or Player vs Player. GitHub Pages supplies HTTPS; PeerJS supplies secure signaling and STUN/TURN-assisted WebRTC connectivity. Internet is required for online rooms. Solo play works offline. The host-controlled Enemy Respawn Rate setting offers Off, Slow, Normal, or Fast replacements in Solo and Co-op; difficulty scales the delay and population cap, while PvP keeps AI disabled.

Fair Play

Solo, Co-op, and PvP use the same weapon timing, movement, collision, spawn protection, gear ownership, and interaction-distance rules. In online matches the host simulates player movement and combat, validates loot and extraction inventory, sanitizes inputs, and limits malformed or repeated network actions.

Firing cancels spawn protection. Repeated invalid actions receive warnings and can be disconnected. Because this is a static peer-hosted browser game, Fair Play reduces casual exploits but cannot equal dedicated-server anti-cheat.
