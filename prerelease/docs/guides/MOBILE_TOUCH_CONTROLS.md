# Mobile touch controls

Critter Extraction v0.27.1 automatically enables its touch interface on phones and tablets. Touchscreen laptops remain in mouse/keyboard mode, and hybrid tablets switch to the most recently used input. You can also force the touch overlay on from **Settings → Always show phone/tablet touch controls**.

## Layout

- **Left joystick:** Move in any direction.
- **Drag to look:** Swipe anywhere in the open right-hand look area for continuous 360° rotation.
- **FIRE / AIM / USE:** Hold these buttons while moving or looking with other fingers.
- **R / + / C:** Reload, heal, and toggle crouch.
- **JUMP / BAG / CAM / ↔ / Ⅱ:** Jump, open inventory, switch first/third person, switch shoulder, and pause.

The layout respects display safe areas and scales down for smaller screens. Landscape orientation is recommended for phones, while both orientations are supported on tablets.

Touch actions reset when the game pauses, the browser loses focus, the page is backgrounded, or a pointer is cancelled, preventing stuck movement, firing, aiming, or interaction.
