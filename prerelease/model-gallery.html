Real Authored 3D Model Gallery

These are original Quaternius CC0 meshes imported as GLB files. They are not the earlier box-and-diamond blockouts. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and use the animation selector on rigged models.

Current state: weapons and selected environment assets are connected to prerelease gameplay. Character models are preserved with their original rigs and animations while the custom game renderer gains skinning and animation playback.
